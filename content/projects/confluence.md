## Machine learning from first principles

Confluence is an interactive platform for learning machine learning visually. It covers 38 algorithms across classification, regression, clustering, and dimensionality reduction — all backed by real scikit-learn computation, not toy math.

Decision boundaries render as interactive heatmaps, hyperparameters retune live, and training can be watched step by step as models actually learn.

## What you can do

- Explore 25 datasets spanning real-world Kaggle data and synthetic generators
- Animate training with loss curves, playback controls, and tree construction steps
- Compare algorithms side by side or race them with live leaderboards
- Click any prediction for decision paths, feature contributions, and explanations
- Generate Python code matching the exact algorithm, dataset, and hyperparameters

## What this proves

- Real ML compute served through a FastAPI engine with Redis caching
- Canvas2D and 3D rendering of decision surfaces, embeddings, and uncertainty
- An education product where every visual is grounded in genuine computation
